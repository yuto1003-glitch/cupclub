# CupClub

_An NFT membership club that turns coffee lovers into a global community_

## Summary

CupClub issues membership NFTs that unlock access to curated coffee drops, voting on which farms/roasters get featured, and in-person meetup perks at partner cafes. Members earn on-chain reputation points for reviews and attendance, redeemable for discounts or rare bean allocations. It turns passive coffee fandom into an active, token-gated community.

## Target users

Coffee enthusiasts, specialty cafes, and roasters wanting a loyal community

## Problem

Coffee lovers lack a shared, portable way to show loyalty and influence across multiple independent cafes/roasters, and loyalty programs are siloed per shop.

## Solution

A single NFT membership works across a network of partner cafes/roasters, with on-chain reputation and governance replacing siloed punch-cards.

## MVP features

- Mint membership NFT tiers with different perks
- DAO-style voting on next featured bean/farm drop
- On-chain reputation points for check-ins and reviews at partner cafes
- Partner cafe dashboard to redeem points/discounts via QR
- Marketplace for limited bean-drop NFTs tied to physical allocation

## Chains

Solana

## Tech

Metaplex, Anchor, Solana Pay, Next.js, Realms (DAO voting), Phantom wallet adapter

## Category

Consumer

## Why now

Token-gated communities and cNFTs are cheap and proven on Solana, and independent cafes increasingly need shared loyalty infrastructure to compete with chains.

## Roadmap

- Onboard a small network of 5-10 partner cafes
- Launch first community-voted limited bean drop
- Add secondary marketplace for membership tier upgrades
